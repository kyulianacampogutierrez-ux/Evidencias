### Slide 2: Evolutionary vs. Revolutionary Technologies in IT
- **Slide Title:** Evolutionary vs. Revolutionary Technological Shifts
- **Estimated Duration:** 0:45 min (Cumulative: 1:20)
- **Visual Focus:** Comparative card layout: Incremental Evolution (left) vs Paradigm Disruption (right).

**Spoken Speech (English):**
> "In software engineering, technological change happens across two primary vectors: evolutionary change and revolutionary breakthroughs.
> On the left, we observe *evolutionary technologies*. These represent steady, incremental improvements. For example, the progression from static HTML to dynamic component-based frameworks like React or modern reactive JavaScript, and the continuous enhancement of CSS design systems. These adaptations refine performance without breaking existing paradigms.
> In contrast, on the right, *revolutionary technologies* disrupt entire industry foundations. Cloud computing completely transformed local physical server rooms into global serverless clouds, while generative AI and automated coding assistants have revolutionized the way developers write, test, and refactor software. Understanding both forces enables us to build resilient applications."

**Spanish Reference:**
> *"En ingeniería de software, el cambio tecnológico ocurre mediante dos vectores primarios: cambio evolutivo e innovaciones revolucionarias..."*

### Slide 5: Grammar Spotlight: Future Perfect & Future Perfect Progressive
- **Slide Title:** Grammar in Context: Future Perfect & Future Perfect Progressive
- **Estimated Duration:** 0:45 min (Cumulative: 3:30)
- **Visual Focus:** Timeline diagrams highlighting `will have + past participle` and `will have been + verb-ing` with concrete IT milestones.

**Spoken Speech (English):**
> "Now, let us examine our grammatical focus: expressing future milestones and continuous processes using the Future Perfect and Future Perfect Progressive tenses.
> First, consider the **Future Perfect** for actions that will be completed before a specific point in time:
> *'By next semester, our team will have deployed the cloud database and will have integrated automated payment gateways into Pizzería Bella Italia.'*
> *'By the time we graduate, we will have developed full-stack enterprise solutions following international clean architecture standards.'*
> Second, observe the **Future Perfect Progressive** for actions continuing up to a future milestone:
> *'By December, I will have been studying software development at SENA for nearly eighteen months.'*
> *'By the end of this sprint, our development team will have been coding reactive user interfaces continuously for three weeks.'*
> Mastering these structures allows technical professionals to clearly communicate roadmaps, deadlines, and project projections."

**Spanish Reference:**
> *"Ahora examinemos nuestro enfoque gramatical: expresar hitos futuros y procesos continuos mediante los tiempos Future Perfect y Future Perfect Progressive..."*
