Future Perfect Tense
Actions Completed Before a Future Time
Grammatical Formula:
Subject + will have + [Past Participle]
1
"By next semester, our team will have deployed the cloud database for Bella Italia."
2
"By graduation, Karen will have developed full-stack enterprise solutions."
3
"The business will have automated 95% of customer orders."
⏳
Future Perfect Progressive
Continuous Duration Leading to a Future Time
Grammatical Formula:
Subject + will have been + [Verb-ing]
1
"By December, I will have been studying at SENA for nearly eighteen months."
2
"By Friday, our sprint team will have been coding the UI for three weeks."
3
"By 2027, companies will have been adopting AI assistants for 5 years."
